Why Is Postnatal Care Important?

Childbirth brings physical and emotional changes. A mother may experience tiredness, changes in sleep, muscle discomfort and the demands of feeding and caring for a newborn. Recovery is different for every woman and can depend on her health, the type of delivery and any complications.

A supportive postnatal routine can help mothers give attention to their wellbeing. Important parts of recovery include:

  • Getting sufficient rest and accepting practical help from family.
  • Eating balanced, nourishing meals and drinking enough fluids.
  • Following medical advice for recovery after vaginal or caesarean delivery.
  • Attending recommended postnatal check-ups.
  • Discussing physical discomfort, sleep concerns or emotional changes with a healthcare professional.

How Ayurveda Approaches Postnatal Care

Traditional Ayurvedic postnatal care in Dubai focuses on warmth, nourishment, rest and a routine suited to the individual. The approach recognises that a mother's needs can change during the recovery period. Food guidance, daily habits and traditional therapies may be discussed as part of a personalised wellness plan.

Any treatment should be selected only after considering the mother's delivery experience, healing, current symptoms and medical advice. Ayurvedic therapies are complementary wellness practices. They do not replace maternity care, medical assessment or treatment prescribed by a doctor.

Traditional Ayurvedic Therapies for New Mothers

Depending on individual assessment and medical clearance, traditional postnatal care discussions may include therapies such as:

Abhyangam

Abhyangam is a traditional Ayurvedic oil massage. The oil, pressure and timing should be chosen carefully for each mother. Massage should not be performed over healing wounds or areas where a healthcare professional has advised against it.

Kizhi

Kizhi is a traditional therapy that uses herbal bundles or compresses. The type of Kizhi, temperature and suitability should be assessed by a qualified practitioner. Heat-based therapies may not be appropriate during every stage of postpartum recovery.

Head Massage

A gentle traditional head massage may be considered as part of a relaxation routine when suitable. The technique and products should be selected according to the mother's comfort and individual needs.

Herbal Bath

Traditional herbal bathing practices may form part of some Ayurvedic postpartum routines. The products and timing should be discussed with the care provider, particularly if the mother has stitches, a surgical wound or skin sensitivity.

Food, Rest and Daily Routine After Delivery

Rest and nourishment are important parts of postpartum recovery. Mothers can discuss a balanced meal plan with their healthcare provider, especially if they are breastfeeding, have food restrictions or are managing a medical condition. Staying hydrated and accepting help with household tasks can also make the early weeks more manageable.

Physical activity should be resumed gradually and only in line with advice from the mother's maternity care provider. There is no single recovery timetable that is right for everyone.

Choosing the Right Postnatal Care in Dubai

When comparing postnatal care options in Dubai, look for a provider that takes time to understand your health and explains each treatment clearly. Before arranging a session, ask:

  • Who will assess your condition and discuss treatment suitability?
  • What does each therapy involve?
  • Which oils or herbal products will be used?
  • When can a treatment safely begin after your delivery?
  • How will the clinic consider medical advice and recovery restrictions?

Share your delivery date, type of delivery, current symptoms and any instructions from your doctor. A responsible provider should not recommend a therapy that conflicts with your medical care.
